Horror Recommender

A free horror movie recommendation tool that helps you find what to watch based on your preferences, horror subgenres, and content warnings.

Currently featuring 60 horror movies, the project welcomes community contributions to expand the database, improve movie classifications, and suggest modifications or new features.

Contributions Welcome!

Have an idea for an improvement? Want to suggest a horror movie or correct a classification? Feel free to open an issue or submit a pull request.

All suggestions, modifications, and improvements are welcome!
